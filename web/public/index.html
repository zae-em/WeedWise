How it works

  1. 01

    Upload a photo

    Upload a clear photo of a single plant, or pick one from the Examples tab.

  2. 02

    Model analysis

    WeedWise Model V2 analyses the photo using what it learned from 48 plant species, including 40 weeds and 8 crops.

  3. 03

    Top 5 matches

    See the five closest matches with confidence scores and whether the model suggests a crop or a weed.

  4. 04

    Learn more

    Open the Species tab for plant information, or ask questions in the Chat tab.
